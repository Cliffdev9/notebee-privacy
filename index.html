Notebee Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

Summary: Notebee stores all your notes locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. Your notes are yours alone.

1. Information We Collect

Notebee is designed with privacy in mind. We collect no personal information from you. Specifically:

  • Notes & Content: All notes, checklists, to-do items, memos, and templates you create are stored exclusively on your device's local storage. We never access, read, or transmit your content.
  • Account Information: Notebee does not require account creation or login.
  • Usage Analytics: We do not use analytics or tracking tools.

2. Data Storage & Backup

Your data is stored locally using an on-device database. Notebee uses Android's built-in Auto Backup feature, which may back up your app data (including notes and preferences) to your personal Google Drive account.

  • This backup is managed entirely by the Android operating system and your Google Account.
  • We (the developer) have no access to your backup data.
  • You can manage or disable this backup in your device's Settings → Google → Backup.

3. Advertising

Notebee displays advertisements provided by AppLovin (MAX). These third-party ad networks may collect certain information to serve relevant ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (advertising ID)
  • General location (country/region level, not precise)
  • Device information (model, OS version)

We do not share your notes or personal content with advertisers. For more details, please review AppLovin's Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions

Notebee may request the following device permissions:

  • Microphone: Used only for voice-to-text note input. Audio is processed on-device and is not recorded or transmitted.
  • Notifications: Used to deliver reminders you set for your notes.
  • Internet: Required for displaying advertisements. Your notes are never sent over the network.

5. Third-Party Services

Notebee integrates the following third-party services:

  • AppLovin MAX — for serving advertisements (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play Services — for Android Auto Backup (Privacy Policy)

6. Children's Privacy

Notebee is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Data Security

Notebee includes a PIN lock feature that allows you to protect individual notes with a password. All note data remains encrypted within Android's application sandbox, which prevents other apps from accessing your data.

8. Your Rights

Since all your data is stored locally on your device, you have full control:

  • Delete: Delete any note or all notes at any time from within the app.
  • Export: Your data remains on your device under your control.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling Notebee removes all locally stored data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
